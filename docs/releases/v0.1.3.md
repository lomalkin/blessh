Security review and reliability release.

**Upgrading**

- `/etc/ble-term/sshd_config` changed (`MaxAuthTries 3`, `LoginGraceTime 60`,
  no forwarding of any kind). If you edited it, dpkg asks whether to keep
  your version; take the new one and reapply your changes.
- The daemon now runs under a D-Bus policy that allows it only the BlueZ
  calls it needs.
- BlueZ older than 5.76 (Debian bookworm): the installer warns that other
  BLE devices nearby can break a session or observe its encrypted traffic.
  Use BlueZ 5.76 or later where that matters.
- Desktop clients (`ble-term-connect`) need EATT off as well
  (`Channels = 1`, the default since BlueZ 5.67); the client warns.
- The upgrade ends an active BLE session.

**Changes**

- Sessions started right after a previous one no longer fail: the daemon
  drops sockets of a link that went down, and leftovers of the previous
  session before HELLO are ignored (protocol rule, compatible with v1).
- Security: peer CLOSE text is escaped in the journal (forged log entries),
  handshake timeout 10 s, D-Bus policy, vulnerability reporting and threat
  model in `docs/SECURITY.md`.
- Protocol: INFO carries the ATT MTU (`mtu=`) and supports long reads; RX
  may be written with or without response; v1 assumes one ATT bearer.
- `ble-term-connect` waits for another instance using the same device.

Tested on Raspberry Pi 3 B+ (trixie), Pi 5 (trixie) and Pi Zero 2 W
(bookworm), with a Pixel 8 Pro and bench to bench.
