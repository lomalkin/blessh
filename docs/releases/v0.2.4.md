A fix for `ble-term-connect` run right after a previous session.

**Fixes**

- `ble-term-connect` started right after the previous one had ended could
  find the old link still going down: the device looked connected, so it
  did not connect, and service discovery failed with "GATT services have
  not been resolved for that Bluetooth device". It now connects again once
  in that case. Seen in 1 of 100 back-to-back sessions from a BlueZ 5.66
  client.
