A second client no longer ends the active session; the daemon recovers
from an adapter that goes away.

**Fixes**

- A client turned away as busy could end the session of the client
  connected before it, a second or two later, with that client's link
  still up. BlueZ closes another device's notification socket when a
  subscribed device disconnects (BlueZ 5.82 and later), and before 5.69
  a second client's writes went into the active stream. INFO now says
  `busy=1` while another device holds the server, and clients that check
  it leave before subscribing: `ble-term-connect` and ConnectBot BLE do.
  Other clients still get CLOSE 1 after subscribing, with the old risk.
  Details in docs/protocol.md, "Why a turned-away client must not
  subscribe".
- When the Bluetooth adapter went away (USB controller unplugged or reset)
  and came back, BlueZ dropped the GATT service and the advertisement and
  the daemon went on without them, silently. It now exits with an error;
  systemd restarts it, and it waits for the adapter and registers again.

**Changes**

- `ble-term-connect` reports "server is busy: another client is connected"
  and exits without subscribing when INFO says `busy=1`.
