A reconnect fix, small fixes and documentation.

**Upgrading**

- sshd's privilege separation directory `/run/sshd` now comes from
  `ble-term-session@.service` (`RuntimeDirectory=sshd`) instead of a
  tmpfiles line. Debian 13 no longer warns about a duplicate tmpfiles
  entry, and BLE sessions keep working after `ssh.service` was stopped
  (stopping it removes `/run/sshd`).

**Fixes**

- A client that ended its session, dropped the link and connected again
  within 10 s could lose the new link about 2 s into the next session: the
  daemon's delayed disconnect of the old link hit the new one. The delayed
  disconnect is now cancelled once the old link is gone. Seen with
  `ble-term-connect` sessions back to back (3 of 25 failed against a
  Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, none after the fix); phones that reconnect on a new
  link were exposed as well.
- The installer creates `/run/sshd` before it checks the sshd
  configuration; without it `sshd -t` failed where ssh.service had never
  run.

**Changes**

- Security notes: password guessing and session slot occupation over BLE
  are accepted risks (docs/SECURITY.md, S-3 and S-4); the README recommends
  public keys only.
- Bench notes: Android 8 and 11 clients, Flipper One stops advertising
  while connected, Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W login times.
